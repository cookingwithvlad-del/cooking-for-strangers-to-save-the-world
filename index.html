COOKING FOR STRANGERS
TO SAVE THE WORLD

Sixty-eight tables. Four cities. Two cooks with a knife roll, a cardboard sign, and a rental car.

Hold up the sign. Get a stranger to say yes. Shop the market, cook in their kitchen, and feed them properly — without asking for anything. Then do it again, from Manhattan to Miami to Chicago to Los Angeles, until the number is ten thousand.
