    How the score works. Each ticker gets six 0–100 readings in the direction of its bias: relative strength against SPY, time-adjusted relative volume, trend (20/50-day averages and 52-week position), Wall Street and insiders (analyst consensus and its change, price-target upside, recent upgrades, insider buying), options and shorts (put/call ratio, unusual options, FINRA short-sale volume, short interest, StockTwits sentiment) and catalysts (earnings, news, fresh 8-Ks, upgrades). The score is their weighted blend: 20 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 15.
    Best data: run node stock-scanner/server.mjs and open http://localhost:8787. The server pulls Yahoo Finance (quotes, pre/post-market, intraday VWAP, options chains, short interest, ownership, analyst targets and upgrades, insider trades, movers), Google News and Google Finance, SEC EDGAR filings, FINRA daily short volume and StockTwits, with no keys. Without the server, the page uses Finnhub with your free key. This is a research tool, not investment advice. Free data can lag, so confirm every level in your broker before you trade.
