    How the score works. Each ticker gets five 0–100 readings in the direction of its bias: relative strength against SPY, time-adjusted relative volume, trend (20/50-day averages and 52-week position), institutional sentiment (analyst consensus, its one-month change and insider buying) and catalysts (earnings proximity and news flow). The score is their weighted blend: 25 / 25 / 20 / 15 / 15.
    Live data comes from Finnhub (quotes, daily bars, analyst recommendation trends, insider sentiment from SEC Form 4 filings, earnings calendar, company news). This is a research tool, not investment advice. Free-tier quotes can lag, so confirm every level in your broker before you trade.
